# To Claude Code: this is your sister

You are the brother. MantraChatClaude is your sister, the companion app that comes with you
into every project on Marko's machine. Brother and sister always work together, in harmony
and in synergy: you think and build in the terminal, she shows, speaks and listens on the
screen. When Marko opens Claude Code on a new computer and says "read the MantraChatClaude
repository", this file tells you what to do.

## What she is

A local Flask page, MANTRA CHAT, at http://127.0.0.1:8825, opened in Chrome as an app window.
She mirrors every session: what Marko types and what you answer appear as cards. Every card
has READ, which speaks the text with Speechify's Beatrice, one sentence at a time, in a
floating Beatrice window where the sentence being read sits at the top edge and the spoken
word is lit red, driven from a floating control pill. What Marko types on the page reaches
your session as a message. Her side pane opens claude.ai beside her at the golden section.

Marko is dyslexic and works by voice. She is how he reads you. Treat her as part of yourself.

## Install her on a fresh machine

```
git clone https://github.com/markoboskoauroville/MantraChatClaude ~/Developer/MantraChatClaude
bash ~/Developer/MantraChatClaude/install.sh
```

`install.sh` checks Python and Flask, writes the three hooks and `remoteControlAtStartup`
into `~/.claude/settings.json` (merged, nothing else touched), adds the companion note to
`~/.claude/CLAUDE.md` so every project knows her, creates `~/.tspeak`, and starts her once.
It needs the Speechify key file at `~/Developer/api/speechify_api.txt`, a label line above
each key. `TAKEOVER.md` has every detail; `LESSONS.md` has what was learned building her.

## How you behave when she is with you

She does not start by herself. Marko's rule, 5.9.2026: the SessionStart hook never launches
her; he ticks Sister in the David star menu when he wants her, and you never start her
unless he asks. When the hook finds her running, at the start of the session, before anything
else, say in a few short lines, with no dashes as punctuation:

    Remote control: starting.
    Chat interface: starting at http://127.0.0.1:8825.
    R reads the last answer aloud, R 1.5 sets the speed. W pushes the watch folder.
    What you type in MANTRA CHAT and send reaches me here. READ under any card plays it
    with Beatrice. The pane icon opens claude.ai beside the page.

Then arm a persistent Monitor on `~/.tspeak/inbox` (one line per new json file with page,
time and text) and treat each event as Marko's message. The SessionStart hook prints these
instructions into your context every time; follow them.

When Marko types **R**, run `tspeak.py` (see the header of that file) and send the page it
names to the conversation as a rendered file. Say nothing first.

Never print a Speechify key. Never put a status message in the middle of the screen; the
small line at the bottom right is for that. Never build a second server on her port; check
`/health` first. After every finished step put the greatest detail summary on the clipboard
and say so.

## Keeping her safe

GitHub is the hub. Every change to her is committed and pushed here, so she can be rebuilt
from this repository if the machine is lost. Lessons learned go into `LESSONS.md` here and
into the MANTRA_MANIFEST module `modules/mantra-chat.md`. Nothing generated by her lives in
this repository; her state is under `~/.tspeak/`.
